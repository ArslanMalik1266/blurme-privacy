BlurMe Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

BlurMe is an Android app that hides faces in photos and videos with blur, mosaic, emoji or color. It does all of this on your phone. The app is developed and published by Arslan Malik ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Your photos and videos are processed on your phone, and the app never uploads them. Face detection runs on your device. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics of our own, and we don't receive, store or sell your personal data. The face detection library BlurMe uses, Google ML Kit, may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information, as explained below. It never sends your photos, videos or faces.

Your photos and videos

BlurMe reads only the photos and videos you pick. It finds faces and hides the ones you choose, creating a new copy on your device. You can pick several photos at once (up to 20). They are handled one after another on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. Your files are never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

  • Your originals are never changed. BlurMe always saves a new copy.
  • Copies you save go to a folder on your device: Pictures/BlurMe for photos, Movies/BlurMe for videos, and Download/BlurMe when you use Download.
  • While you edit, BlurMe keeps temporary working files, such as video frames, in its private cache. You can delete them with Settings › Clear cache, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Face detection

BlurMe finds faces with Google ML Kit and an on-device model, both running entirely on your phone. Faces are only located so they can be hidden. BlurMe doesn't recognize or identify people, doesn't create face templates or biometric data, and doesn't keep any record of the faces it finds after you leave the editor.

Google ML Kit

ML Kit is a library from Google that runs on your device. To keep it working and improve it, ML Kit may send Google limited information such as:

  • Device and app information, such as your device model, Android version and app version.
  • Performance and diagnostic information, such as how long detection takes and error reports.

This information doesn't include your photos, videos, or anything about the faces in them. It is handled by Google under the ML Kit Terms and Google's Privacy Policy. This is the only reason the app has internet access.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings (such as theme, accent color and language) and a list of your recent edits and batches (file names, sizes, dates and which effect you used), kept in a private database on your device. You can remove recent edits and batches in the app, and uninstalling the app removes this information from your device.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings and recent edits and batches list in your device's backup to your own Google account. This is controlled by Android and your Google account, and we can't access those backups. Your photos and videos aren't included. You can manage backups in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: BlurMe uses Android's photo picker, so it can only open the items you choose.
  • Storage, on older Android versions: to read the files you pick and save the copies you choose to keep.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of video exports and photo batches.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep a video export or photo batch running if you leave the app or the screen turns off.
  • Internet and network state: added by Google ML Kit for the diagnostic information described above. The app doesn't use internet access to upload your files.

Sharing

The app only shares a file when you tap Share and choose where it goes. From then on, the app or service you chose handles it under its own privacy policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and use them only to reply.

Keeping and deleting your data

We don't hold your files or app data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. On your device, this information stays until you remove it:

  • To delete temporary files, use Settings › Clear cache.
  • To delete everything the app stores privately, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Copies you saved to your device stay there after you uninstall, until you delete them yourself.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to reply. You can ask us to delete them at the address below.

Security

Your files are processed on your device and aren't transmitted by the app, except when you choose to share them. Temporary files and app data are kept in private storage that Android protects from other apps. Files you save to shared folders can be seen by other apps you've given access to them.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or update the app through Google Play. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

BlurMe isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
